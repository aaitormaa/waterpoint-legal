WaterPoint — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

WaterPoint helps cyclists find drinking water along their route. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, who receives it, and your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French Data Protection Act.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Aitor Martin Fernandez de Leceta, Bordeaux, France.
Contact for privacy questions: tormar93@gmail.com

2. Summary

  • No account, no email, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no sale of data.
  • A public name is optional. If you choose one, it is shown with your score in the ranking.
  • The GPX routes you open stay on your device. They are never uploaded.
  • Your location is used only on your device, only when you ask for it (“Water near me” or Live mode). It is never uploaded.
  • If you add a water point or report that one works or is dry, that information is stored in a shared database. Added points are visible to other users.

5. Community content

Water points you add and the notes you write are public. Do not write personal information in notes. Reports (works / dry) are used only in aggregate (counts over the last 90 days); individual reports and device identifiers are not shown to other users. To have a point removed, contact the address in section 1 with its location and approximate date.

5b. Public name and ranking (optional)

You may choose a public name (2 to 20 characters) to appear in the ranking with your score. The score is calculated from the water points you added (10 points each) and your reports (1 point each). The name is linked to a random device identifier, not to your identity. Do not use your real name, an email address or any personal data. To remove your name from the ranking, save an empty name in the Profile tab, or contact the address in section 1. Offensive names will be removed.

Recovery code. When you create a profile the app shows you a random recovery code once. We store only a one-way hash of it (never the code itself) so that you can restore your profile and score on a new phone. We do not collect your email address. If you lose the code, we cannot recover the profile.

6. Legal basis

  • Providing the app you requested (contract): maps, routes, search, water points.
  • Consent: device location permission, publishing community content and choosing a public name; you can withdraw it any time in your device settings.
  • Legitimate interest: keeping the community data accurate and preventing abuse.

7. Retention

Data on your device stays until you delete it. Community points stay until removed (on request or if inaccurate or unlawful). Reports are kept to compute the 90-day status and may be deleted afterwards. Your public name is kept until you remove it.

8. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent. Because the app uses no account, we can only identify data linked to a device identifier if you send it to us. Write to the contact in section 1. You may also complain to the CNIL or to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

WaterPoint is not directed at children under 15 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

10. Security and changes

Connections use HTTPS. If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
